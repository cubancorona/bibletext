BibleText

A clean, fast, distraction-free way to read and study the Bible.

  • 📖Distraction-free reading — the World English Bible (incl. a Catholic edition), Berean Standard Bible, and New King James Version — words of Christ in red, light & dark themes.
  • 🤖AI study — select any passage and have it explained, its context unpacked, or its translation discussed. Bring your own AI key; entirely optional.
  • 📤Share — any verse as a clean, typeset image card, or as text with a Bluebook-style citation.
  • 💬Notes from friends — send a verse with a short note riding inside the link. Click or tap it — the note opens beside the passage, in the app if you have it, or (for the public-domain translations) right in the browser. No account, no server.
  • 🎧Narration — complete human-recorded audio on every platform, with read-along verse highlighting; on-device read-aloud on iPhone, iPad, Android, and Mac, where audio also keeps playing with the screen locked.

Opening it the first time

macOS blocks the app’s first launch until you allow it. On macOS 15 and later, open the app and dismiss the warning, then, within the hour, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, click Open Anyway and confirm with your login password. On macOS 12 to 14, Control-click the app in the Finder, choose Open, then click Open in the warning.
