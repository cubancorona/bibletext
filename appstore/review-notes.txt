VERSION 1.2.4

BibleText is a Bible reader. No account, login, or registration is required. Reading, search, navigation, notes, and cross-references do not require an AI-provider key. Downloaded scripture remains readable and searchable offline; network-backed downloads and optional provider services require connectivity when used.

WHAT IS NEW IN 1.2.3
Red-letter text now follows each translation's own publisher markings instead of borrowing another edition's decisions.
• NKJV restores four sayings its publisher marks as Christ's words but the WEB does not: Mark 5:31, Matthew 27:63, Luke 17:36, and Luke 24:7.
• WEB, WEB Catholic, BSB, and NKJV each use their own marked spans, including partial verses.
• A source-text revision can no longer silently colour the wrong words: a mismatched span safely degrades within that same edition.

HOW TO EXERCISE 1.2.3
Open Settings → Reading and leave Words of Jesus enabled. Select NKJV, then open Mark 5:31, Matthew 27:63, Luke 17:36, or Luke 24:7; the spoken words are red. Switch among WEB, WEB Catholic, and BSB at a Gospel passage to see that each edition keeps its own markings. Toggle Words of Jesus off and on to confirm the setting applies immediately.

CURRENT NAVIGATION
Read, Books, and Search sit at the bottom on iPhone and iPad portrait. On iPad landscape, the same three destinations move to a rail on the left. Books is a grouped Old Testament / New Testament grid.

SHARED NOTES — HOW TO TEST
Shared notes are unchanged in 1.2.3 and are described because they are the one place the app displays text written by another person.

To send: open any chapter, press and hold to select a verse, choose Share with note, type a short message, and share it through any app. The result is an ordinary https://bibletext.co.uk link.

To receive: tap the link. The app opens the passage with the message beside it. Opening a public-domain link in a browser shows the passage and note; an NKJV link shows a no-text licensing notice, the note, public-domain parallels, and a route into the app.

PRIVACY: the message is encoded in the URL fragment and travels only through the sender's chosen messaging app. There is no server, account, user directory, or developer copy. The fragment is not sent to the website host. A received message is rendered as plain text with neutral attribution, never as markup or in the app's voice. The recipient can delete one note, delete all notes in Settings, or disable notes entirely.

KEYS AND TRANSLATIONS
The App Store binary intentionally contains an obfuscated, link-time-injected API.Bible fallback used only to fetch the licensed New King James Version. A reader can replace it with their own API.Bible key. The NKJV text itself is fetched from API.Bible and is not packaged in the app or published on the website.

No AI-provider key is bundled. Optional Study with AI and Find features remain off until the reader enters their own provider key in Settings → Assistant. That key is stored on the device and requests go directly to the selected provider. No AI setup is required for review.

The full WEB, WEB Catholic, and BSB texts are fetched and cached at runtime. The only embedded scripture is a small WEB Gospels seed used to show readable text immediately on first launch; the embedded Gospel-parallels dataset contains references rather than another full translation.
