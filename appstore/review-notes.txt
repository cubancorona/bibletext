VERSION 1.2.8

BibleText is a Bible reader. No account, login, or registration is required. Reading, search, navigation, notes, and cross-references do not require an AI-provider key. Downloaded scripture remains readable and searchable offline; network-backed downloads and optional provider services require connectivity when used.

WHAT IS NEW IN 1.2.7
• Landscape reading on iPhone: turning the phone sideways on the Read tab fills the screen with the chapter — no header, no tab bar — with the chapter arrows beside the reference. Turning back restores the usual chrome.
• Section headings now appear where the publisher set them, and paragraphs break where the publisher broke them, in every translation rather than on a rule of our own.
• A new reading face, Junicode, with Ezra SIL for Hebrew. The divine name is set in small capitals as the editions intend, and copying a verse still gives ordinary letters.
• Psalm titles — the superscriptions that name a psalm's author or occasion — are now shown in the New King James Version.
• Footnotes gather at the foot of the chapter, so a marker in the text has somewhere to take you.
• Selecting a verse that carries a highlight now shows the selection; it was painted underneath.

HOW TO EXERCISE 1.2.7
On an iPhone, open the Read tab and rotate to landscape: the chapter fills the screen and the chapter arrows sit beside the reference; rotate back and the header and tab bar return. Open Psalm 3 in the New King James Version to see the superscription above verse 1. Open any chapter in any translation to see the publisher's own section headings and paragraph breaks, and scroll to the foot of the chapter for its footnotes. Press and hold to select a verse that carries a highlight and confirm the selection is visible over it.

CURRENT NAVIGATION
Read, Books, and Search sit at the bottom on iPhone and iPad portrait. On iPad landscape the same three destinations move to a rail on the left. On iPhone landscape Books and Search keep the bottom tab bar, and the Read tab reads full-screen. Books is a grouped Old Testament / New Testament grid.

SHARED NOTES — HOW TO TEST
Shared notes are the one place the app displays text written by another person.

To send: open any chapter, press and hold to select a verse, choose Share with note, type a short message, and share it through any app. The result is an ordinary https://bibletext.co.uk link.

To receive: tap the link. The app opens the passage with the message beside it. Opening a public-domain link in a browser shows the passage and note; an NKJV link shows a no-text licensing notice, the note, public-domain parallels, and a route into the app.

PRIVACY: the message is encoded in the URL fragment and travels only through the sender's chosen messaging app. There is no server, account, user directory, or developer copy. The fragment is not sent to the website host. A received message is rendered as plain text with neutral attribution, never as markup or in the app's voice. The recipient can delete one note, delete all notes in Settings, or disable notes entirely.

KEYS AND TRANSLATIONS
The App Store binary intentionally contains an obfuscated, link-time-injected API.Bible fallback used only to fetch the licensed New King James Version. A reader can replace it with their own API.Bible key. The NKJV text itself is fetched from API.Bible and is not packaged in the app or published on the website.

No AI-provider key is bundled. Optional Study with AI and Find features remain off until the reader enters their own provider key in Settings → Assistant. That key is stored in the device Keychain and requests go directly to the selected provider. No AI setup is required for review.

The full WEB, WEB Catholic, and BSB texts are fetched and cached at runtime. The only embedded scripture is a small WEB Gospels seed used to show readable text immediately on first launch; the embedded Gospel-parallels dataset contains references rather than another full translation.
