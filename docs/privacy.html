BibleText — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026

BibleText is a Bible reading and study app, built to respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

The short version

BibleText has no user accounts, shows no ads, and does not track or sell your data. The developer runs no server that receives it. If you enable an optional AI feature, the app sends only the request described below directly to the provider account you selected.

Information stored on your device

Your reading position, history, and display settings are stored only on your device. On Apple devices, any AI provider key you choose to add is stored in the system Keychain; an existing key saved by an older BibleText release is moved there automatically. These values are not transmitted to the developer.

Bible text, study data, and audio

Bible translations and study data are downloaded from public, free services so the app can display scripture: bible.helloao.org (the primary source for the World English Bible — including the Catholic edition — and the Berean Standard Bible), bible-api.com (a fallback text source), and openbible.info (cross-reference data). If you play a chapter's audio, the public-domain recordings are streamed from the project's own public mirror hosted on GitHub (originally recorded for openbible.com — Berean Standard Bible narration — and ebible.org — World English Bible recordings); the on-device read-aloud option uses your device's speech synthesis and makes no network request. These are ordinary network requests for the text, study data, and audio themselves; no personal information about you is attached to them.

Optional AI study features (bring your own key)

BibleText's AI features — Find, Explain, and context or translation analysis — are optional and active only if you add your own API key for a third-party AI provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, or xAI/Grok). When you use one of these features, your Find search or the passage and study action you selected are sent directly from your device to the provider you chose, using your own key, so it can generate a response. The provider may associate that request with your provider account and retain it under its own privacy policy and terms. BibleText does not route the request through any server of ours, does not use it for tracking, and does not retain your prompts after the app closes.

For App Store privacy disclosure, BibleText is listed as Data Not Collected: the developer operates no server, no accounts, and no analytics, and never receives any of this data. Text you choose to send to your own AI provider goes directly to that provider under your own key and their policies — it is your disclosure to your own vendor, not collection by this app's developer.

Your choices, retention, and deletion

You can use every core reading feature without AI. Choose Assistant → None to stop AI features, and use Clear beside a provider key to remove that key from the Apple Keychain. Removing the app clears its ordinary reading preferences and cached data; clear provider keys in the app first if you also want those credentials removed.

BibleText keeps AI results only in memory until the app closes. An AI provider may retain requests under your account terms. Use that provider's account controls or contact the provider for access or deletion; the BibleText developer cannot view or delete data held in your provider account.

Children's privacy

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. AI cannot be used unless the user supplies a provider account key, and that provider's age requirements and terms apply.
