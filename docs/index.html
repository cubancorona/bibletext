BibleText

A clean, fast, distraction-free way to read and study the Bible.

  • 📖Distraction-free reading of the World English Bible, its Catholic edition with the deuterocanon, and the Berean Standard Bible — words of Christ in red on iPhone, iPad, Android, and Mac; light & dark themes.
  • 📜Poetry as poetry — the Psalms, Proverbs, Job and the prophets' oracles set in their authored verse lines, one poetic line per line, as in print. Shared and copied text keeps those lines.
  • 🤖AI study — select any passage and have it explained, its context unpacked, or its translation discussed. Bring your own AI key; entirely optional.
  • 📤Share — any verse as a clean, typeset image card, or as text with a Bluebook-style citation.
  • 🎧Narration — complete human-recorded audio on every platform; read-along verse highlighting and on-device read-aloud on iPhone, iPad, Android, and Mac, where audio also keeps playing with the screen locked.
