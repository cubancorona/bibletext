New King James Version

A Call to Repentance

1 In the eighth month of the second year of Darius, the word of the Lᴏʀᴅ came to Zechariah the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo the prophet, saying, 2 “The Lᴏʀᴅ has been very angry with your fathers. 3 Therefore say to them, ‘Thus says the Lᴏʀᴅ of hosts: “Return to Me,” says the Lᴏʀᴅ of hosts, “and I will return to you,” says the Lᴏʀᴅ of hosts. 4 “Do not be like your fathers, to whom the former prophets preached, saying, ‘Thus says the Lᴏʀᴅ of hosts: “Turn now from your evil ways and your evil deeds.” ’ But they did not hear nor heed Me,” says the Lᴏʀᴅ.
5 “Your fathers, where are they?
And the prophets, do they live forever?
6 Yet surely My words and My statutes,
Which I commanded My servants the prophets,
Did they not overtake your fathers? “So they returned and said:
‘Just as the Lᴏʀᴅ of hosts determined to do to us,
According to our ways and according to our deeds,
So He has dealt with us.’ ” ’ ”

Vision of the Horses

7 On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, which is the month Shebat, in the second year of Darius, the word of the Lᴏʀᴅ came to Zechariah the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo the prophet: 8 I saw by night, and behold, a man riding on a red horse, and it stood among the myrtle trees in the hollow; and behind him were horses: red, sorrel, and white. 9 Then I said, “My lord, what are these?” So the angel who talked with me said to me, “I will show you what they are.”

10 And the man who stood among the myrtle trees answered and said, “These are the ones whom the Lᴏʀᴅ has sent to walk to and fro throughout the earth.”

11 So they answered the Angel of the Lᴏʀᴅ, who stood among the myrtle trees, and said, “We have walked to and fro throughout the earth, and behold, all the earth is resting quietly.”

The Lᴏʀᴅ Will Comfort Zion

12 Then the Angel of the Lᴏʀᴅ answered and said, “O Lᴏʀᴅ of hosts, how long will You not have mercy on Jerusalem and on the cities of Judah, against which You were angry these seventy years?”

13 And the Lᴏʀᴅ answered the angel who talked to me, with good and comforting words.
14 So the angel who spoke with me said to me, “Proclaim, saying, ‘Thus says the Lᴏʀᴅ of hosts:
“I am zealous for Jerusalem
And for Zion with great zeal.
15 I am exceedingly angry with the nations at ease;
For I was a little angry,
And they helped—but with evil intent.”

16 ‘Therefore thus says the Lᴏʀᴅ:
“I am returning to Jerusalem with mercy;
My house shall be built in it,” says the Lᴏʀᴅ of hosts,
“And a surveyor’s line shall be stretched out over Jerusalem.” ’

17 “Again proclaim, saying, ‘Thus says the Lᴏʀᴅ of hosts:
“My cities shall again spread out through prosperity;
The Lᴏʀᴅ will again comfort Zion,
And will again choose Jerusalem.” ’ ”

Vision of the Horns

18 Then I raised my eyes and looked, and there were four horns. 19 And I said to the angel who talked with me, “What are these?” So he answered me, “These are the horns that have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”

20 Then the Lᴏʀᴅ showed me four craftsmen. 21 And I said, “What are these coming to do?” So he said, “These are the horns that scattered Judah, so that no one could lift up his head; but the craftsmen are coming to terrify them, to cast out the horns of the nations that lifted up their horn against the land of Judah to scatter it.”
